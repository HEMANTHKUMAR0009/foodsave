How FoodSave Works

A simple, zero-friction 3-step loop connecting food surpluses with community hunger.

1

Post Surplus Food

Dining halls, kitchens, or hosts publish food details, pickup instructions, and expiry deadline in under 60 seconds.

2

Instant Claim & Verification

Shelters, pantries, or students reserve the food with 1 click and receive a verified Digital Pickup Pass with code.

3

Pickup & Track Impact

Collect the food cleanly. The system logs meals rescued, carbon offset, and waste diverted from landfills.
