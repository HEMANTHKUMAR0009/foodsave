The Challenge

The Real-World Crisis

Millions of tons of edible food end up in landfills while local communities face hunger.

🗑️
Closing-Time Waste
College canteens, hostel messes, restaurants, and caterers prepare food in bulk. Every night at closing, large edible batches are thrown away.
⏳
The Coordination Bottleneck
Providers lack a rapid, automated way to log remaining food. Shelters and NGOs have zero real-time visibility to dispatch volunteers before food spoils.
🌍
Massive Carbon Cost
Landfilled food rots into methane—a potent greenhouse gas. Every 1 kg of wasted food produces ~2.5 kg of CO₂ equivalent emissions.

Our Solution

Introducing FoodSave

An end-to-end intelligent platform connecting surplus food providers with receivers in under 60 seconds.

Game Changer Feature

Closing-Time Auto-Dispatch

Solving the exact scenario: Restaurants & canteens closing at night automatically alert NGOs in 1 minute.

Step 1
Operating Hours & Menu
Configure business hours (e.g. 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM). The live inventory tracker tallies prepared batches vs portions sold for each dish.
Step 2
Automated Surplus Audit
At closing time, the system automatically audits remaining food, aggregates total meals & weight, and checks safe storage compliance.
Step 3
60-Second NGO Countdown
Triggers an automated 1-minute countdown timer. At 00:00, automated broadcast dispatch alerts are pushed to registered partner charities and shelters.

Algorithmic Intelligence

Smart Rescue Priority Engine

Dynamic urgency scoring guarantees that food closest to expiry gets rescued first.

🔥 HIGH PRIORITY
Urgent Perishables
Condition: Expires in ≤ 2 hours OR quantity > 40 meals.

Prominently badged in red and automatically pinned to the top of the search feed for immediate rescue pickup.
⚡ MEDIUM PRIORITY
Moderate Window
Condition: Expires in ≤ 5 hours OR quantity between 20–40 meals.

Badged in golden amber; targeted for scheduled pickup rounds by local volunteer drivers.
🟢 LOW PRIORITY
Standard Window
Condition: Ample expiry window (> 5 hours) & standard meal size.

Badged in emerald green; ideal for pantry consolidation or next-morning distributions.
⏱️ Live Dynamic Ticker: Every 30 seconds, background interval audits timestamps. If a Medium item enters the 2-hour window, it automatically upgrades to HIGH PRIORITY in real time.

Seamless UX

Claiming & Digital Pickup Pass

A friction-free workflow designed for quick operations in high-stress volunteer environments.

🎫 Digital Pickup Pass Features
  • 🔒 Cryptographic 6-Digit Claim Code: Unique token (e.g. FS-7491) prevents unauthorized claims.
  • 📞 1-Click Call Provider: Direct phone call link to the kitchen manager.
  • 📋 One-Tap Share Instructions: Copies pickup address, gate instructions, and contact details for dispatch drivers.
  • ✅ Proof of Pickup: Receiver taps "Confirm Food Picked Up" to close the claim loop.

Prompt to Production Track

AI & Prompt Architecture

How structured prompt design powers rapid surplus valuation, classification, and partner alerts.

🤖 Prompt Engineering Pipelines
  • 1. Surplus Extraction: Ingests raw kitchen inventory logs and extracts item names, meal counts, and estimated kg weight using strict JSON schemas.
  • 2. Food-Safety Temperature Advisor: Automatically attaches safety guidelines (e.g. "Maintain hot hold >65°C" or "Refrigerate at <4°C").
  • 3. Contextual NGO Alert Generator: Crafts personalized dispatch alerts with pickup time windows, allergen flags, and vehicle size recommendations.

"role": "system",
